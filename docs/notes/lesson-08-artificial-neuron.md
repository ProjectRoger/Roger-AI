1. Why do neurons have weights?

Neurons have weights because every Input can have different impact on the final output.

2. What is a weighted sum?

Weighted Sum is the total sum of the Weights multiplied by the inputs respectively.

3. Why can't we stop after the weighted sum?

If we stop after the weighted Sum then we cannot capture the relationship as weighted sum can be same every time.

4. What does an activation function do?

An Activation is used to capture linear or non-linear relationshi present between the inputs.

5. Why aren't all inputs equally important?

All the inputs are not equally important as some inputs have greater impact over the output while some inputs have relatively less impact on the output.

6. Why are many neurons more powerful than one?

Single Neuron captures only one relationship, if there are multiple neurons then we can capture multiple relationships present between the inputs.
